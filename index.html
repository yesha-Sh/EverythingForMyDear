Surat Cinta Untukmu

🌹

For My Lovely One,

HAII KAMUUU!!! my pretty lovely girl 💐
Kamu tahu? Every time I see you, I always feel happy.
Like… look at my adorable, beautiful girl.
I love you so much.
Rasanya dunia langsung jadi lebih ringan cuma karena lihat senyummu.
Aku harap kehadiranku bisa membuat harimu sedikit lebih baik,
sedikit lebih hangat, dan jauh lebih bahagia.
Because if I can be even a small reason behind your smile,
that would already mean everything to me.
My dear… I’m so proud of you.
For everything you do. For how strong you are. For how you keep going even when things get hard. And I love everything about you —
the way you think,
the way you talk,
the way you care,
even the little things you mungkin anggap biasa saja…
but to me, they are extraordinary.

Aku tahu bagaimana dunia memperlakukanmu.
I know it’s not always kind.
Kadang capek.
Kadang berat.
Kadang terasa tidak adil.
But no matter how exhausting it gets, please remember this —
I will always be the first one to support you.
I will stand beside you when you walk.
Run with you when you chase your dreams.
And hold you when you feel like you can’t move anymore.
I’ll help you reach everything you desire.
Everything for you, sweetheart.

Honestly…
di hari Valentine ini aku tidak terlalu memikirkannya. I didn’t even plan to celebrate it. But today, I choose to celebrate you. Because for me, Valentine is not about flowers or fancy things —
it’s about the person who makes my heart feel full. And that person is you,
my pretty princess.

And there’s something important I want to say.
You know that I love you…
and I know we love each other.
But sometimes it feels strange, doesn’t it? We care this deeply,
yet we don’t have a clear name for what we are.
Rasanya seperti dua hati yang sudah saling menemukan,
tapi belum berani mengikat janji.
So… my dear —
Let me be your last one.
Let me be your light every time you’re feeling down.
Let me be your home when the world feels too loud.

Let me stay —
not just for today, not just for Valentine’s Day, but for every ordinary day after this.
Devita… would you be mine, forever and ever? 💗

with all of my love,

yours forever
